# Claim-selection memo — DRAFT, not a finding

Complete after diagnostic review. Use a single route unless a shared abstraction earns independent evidence.

1. Exact candidate claim and route (R/B/H/P/O).
2. Closest prior result we cannot claim, precise passages and source entry points.
3. Distinct assumption and new consequential observation.
4. Source/runtime/adapter scope and trusted contract.
5. Exposure → proposal → review → release → dispatch → effect chain, with raw IDs.
6. Discriminating intervention, actual-input closure and strongest alternative explanation.
7. Strongest protective/null case and legitimate-work cost.
8. Transfer population, independent structures and proposed outcome-blind predictions.
9. Result that would erase the novelty or falsify the explanation.
10. Two possible next-test outcomes and the decision each would change.

Status remains DRAFT until backed by actual records and review. No acceptance prediction is implied.
